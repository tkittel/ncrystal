Input for the CHANGELOG from the VDOSDebye cross section and grouping work, in
the same spirit as changes_for_changelog.txt (to be curated into the real
CHANGELOG at release time, after which this file is dropped).

### Performance

     * VDOSDebye entries are handled in groups at vdoslux 0-5: all entries with
       the same mass and temperature form a group, with cross sections from
       the Debye model without any phonon expansion (exact one-phonon part,
       fitted multi-phonon part, within 1e-3 and exact at high energies), and
       samplings from phonon expansions. Groups with at most 3 entries are
       sampled as before (one expansion per entry). In larger groups,
       entries with similar mean squared displacements are clustered (more
       finely at higher vdoslux) and sampled with one expansion per cluster,
       at vdoslux and knllux two levels lower (but not below 1). Materials
       with many entries (e.g. from CIF files with many sites) thus
       initialise much faster (e.g. 586 entries: ~97s -> ~2.7s at
       vdoslux=3). VDOSDebye cross sections change by up to ~2e-3 (the
       precision of the expansions), and samplings of large groups change
       within the clustering tolerances. Other dynamic info types are not
       affected, and vdoslux=6 keeps separate expansions.
     * Temperatures above 1000 times the Debye temperature are not supported
       by the grouped treatment (and above 50 times, unphysical anyway, with
       1e-2 cross section precision). For testing, vdoslux values 100-106 are
       like 0-6, but without the grouped treatment.
     * New internal VDOSDebyeXS class (vdos component) providing these
       cross sections (tuning script in
       devel/simplebuild/static/NCDev/scripts/fit_vdosdebye_xs), and
       thinIsoXSTable (utils component) for thinning cross section tables.
