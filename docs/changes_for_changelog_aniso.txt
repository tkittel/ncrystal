Input for the CHANGELOG from the tk_aniso branch (anisotropic displacements),
in the same spirit as changes_for_changelog.txt (to be curated into the real
CHANGELOG at release time, after which this file is dropped).

### Anisotropic displacements

     * NCMAT v8 @ATOMSITES lines can end with "uij" and six numbers, the
       anisotropic displacement tensor of the site in the CIF convention
       (U11,U22,U33,U12,U13,U23). The tensors only give the shapes: each is
       scaled so its Ueq equals the msd from @DYNINFO (sites with different
       msds need separate labels). Tensors must be given for all or none of
       the sites of an element, must be positive definite, and are snapped to
       the site symmetry (clear breaks are rejected).
     * Bragg structure factors use anisotropic Debye-Waller factors, and the
       incoherent elastic model the powder average over directions of the
       projected msd. Inelastic scattering stays isotropic. No change for
       materials without tensors.
     * AtomInfo::Site::uij holds the tensors (mapped to the representative
       position, symmetrised and scaled), also available in the "sites" JSON
       query, the Python AtomInfo.sites, and the dumps.
     * NCMATComposer always writes NCMAT v8 data (non-crystalline materials
       therefore need set_state_of_matter, unless implied by the dynamics),
       set_cellsg accepts space group settings like "227:2", and the new
       set_atomsites takes one position per site, optionally with an
       occupancy and/or a uij tensor. Site occupancies are written to
       @ATOMOCCUPANCIES.
     * NCMATComposer.refine_crystal_structure keeps sites with uij tensors
       (transformed to the refined cell). Site tensors enter the symmetry
       detection via their eigenvalues, and are dropped with a warning if
       those of equivalent sites are not each other's symmetry images.
     * NCMATComposer.refine_crystal_structure and .verify_crystal_structure
       have new tolerance parameters tol, tol_symprec (and tol_uij for
       refinement), limited to 5% (relative) and 0.5Aa. The symprec
       parameter is an obsolete alias of tol_symprec, and all parameters
       are now keyword-only.
     * cif2ncmat and CIFLoader keep anisotropic displacements, also when
       spglib refines the structure (e.g. from P1 or primitive cells), with
       the tensors transformed accordingly. Sites given separately are only
       considered equivalent if their compositions, occupancies, Uiso values
       and tensor eigenvalues agree within tolerances (new CIFLoader
       parameters tol, tol_symprec, tol_uij, tol_occu, and cif2ncmat options
       --tol, --tol-symprec, --tol-uij, --tol-occu), and tensors are dropped
       with a warning if those of equivalent sites are not each other's
       symmetry images. CIFLoader.atoms no longer has an 'aniso' key, and the
       new CIFLoader.sites gives one entry per site, with the tensor.
     * CIF data listing symmetry operations which form a group, but not
       that of a tabulated space group setting (e.g. symmetrised primitive
       cells written by pymatgen), are expanded with those operations.
     * CIFLoader and cif2ncmat reject CIF data with inconsistent symmetry
       information (listed symmetry operations not matching the space group
       symbol or being incomplete or unparsable, site multiplicities not
       matching the symmetry, anisotropic displacements of equivalent sites
       which are not each other's symmetry images). The new permissive mode
       (CIFLoader permissive parameter, cif2ncmat --permissive) loads some of
       these anyway with warnings, when the needed information can be
       recovered. Cell parameters slightly off the declared symmetry are
       accepted (with a warning) if refinement confirms the space group.
     * cif2ncmat warns when a Uiso differs from the Ueq of the tensor by more
       than 5%. The Ueq of tensors without Uiso is now correct for
       non-orthogonal cells (it was the mean of the diagonal).
